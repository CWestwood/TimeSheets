Persal Details
  • Col A — Full name
  • Col B — Persal number
  • Col C — Job title / rank
  • Col D — Appointment date
  • Col E - Nature of Appointment
  • Col F — Overtime Group
  • Col G - Contract End (for contract/CSMOs, otherwise N/A)
Leave Schedule
  • Col A — Name
  • Col B — Leave type (Annual, Sick, Family Responsibility, Other)
  • Col C — Start date
  • Col D — End date
Call Roster
  • Col A — Date
  • Col C — OT start time
  • Col D — OT end time
  • Col E/F/G — On-call names
  • Col H — Standby name
Public Holidays
  • Col A — Holiday date
